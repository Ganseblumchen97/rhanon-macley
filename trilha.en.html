Soundtrack

All of the game's music is composed in code and played in real time by the browser, with no audio files. Each character and each region has its own theme, a leitmotif: the same melody comes back in different arrangements, in the city, in battle and against the boss, to tell the story through sound. The idea came from Toby Fox's work on Deltarune. It sounds like the 16-bit consoles with a modern mix: FM as on the Mega Drive, the Super Nintendo's echo and Brazilian instruments built by physical modeling.

Characters

Rhanon's theme title screen

It opens with a leap of a fourth, like a spear thrust, and keeps climbing. Acid jazz with a syncopated bass.

Pytuna's theme the villain

Low and slow: it rises a half step, leaps the tritone and lands on a note that never resolves. Smoke that never settles.

Jaci's theme the moon

A leap of a sixth that slowly falls and stops on a floating D: hope, not victory. It plays when a region is calmed.

Regions

North forest battle

The North theme in the São Bento Grande rhythm, the fast capoeira circle, with atabaque drum, pandeiro and flute.

Northeast São Luís

A bumba-meu-boi song mixed with forró: matraca clappers and big pandeiro underneath, accordion and triangle on top.

Center-West Cuiabá

A country "toada": plucked viola caipira, accordion in the back and the theme as a duet in thirds, like a sertanejo duo.

Southeast Minas Gerais

Modern sertanejo: steel-string guitar with a pop strum, claps and the theme sung as a duo. São Paulo gets MPB, and Rio, funk.

Boss in every region

It opens with war drums and a bell toll, then moves on with distorted guitar.

Excerpts recorded from the game itself. Each region has more arrangements of the same theme: village, map, night, water and city.
